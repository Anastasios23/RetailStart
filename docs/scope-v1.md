# V1 Scope

## In scope

- Products
- Categories
- Product variants such as size and color
- Inventory
- Cart
- Checkout
- Payments
- Orders
- Basic shipping
- Admin functionality for products and orders

## Not in scope

- ERP/POS integrations
- Subscriptions
- B2B pricing
- Restaurant-specific functionality
- Marketplaces
- Complex promotions

## TBD

- Detailed checkout and payment flows
- Shipping rules, providers, and regions
- Inventory rules and low-stock behavior
- Admin roles and permissions
- Customer account behavior
- Tax, returns, notifications, search, and reporting requirements
- V1 acceptance criteria and non-functional requirements
