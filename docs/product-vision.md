# Product Vision

## Purpose

Create a reusable foundation for launching retail e-commerce websites for different client businesses.

## Initial users and market

The initial target is normal retail businesses that sell physical products online.

## Reusability goal

The starter should provide a common retail foundation that can be adapted per client while keeping each client deployment isolated.

## V1 product direction

V1 should support the core journey from browsing products through cart, checkout, payment, and order management, with basic inventory and shipping support.

## Boundaries

The first version is not intended to cover ERP/POS integrations, subscriptions, B2B pricing, restaurant-specific functionality, marketplaces, or complex promotions.

Detailed client requirements and future expansion priorities are `TBD`.
