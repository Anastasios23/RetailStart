## Reputation

Google-style public reviews: **5.0 / 5** (~26 reviews). Themes: friendly staff, trustworthy dispensing, easy access, clean shop.

## What they sell online (Wolt)

Delivery catalog emphasizes **dermocosmetics, wellness, OTC care** — not a prescription e-pharmacy. Brands include AHAVA, APIVITA, AVENE, CeraVe, Frezyderm, Korres, La Roche-Posay, The Ordinary, Vichy, Uriage, Caudalie, SVR, New Nordic, etc.

Category examples: face/body care, hair, supplements, first aid, dental, baby/kids, cold & flu, sunscreens, orthopedics accessories.

Most-ordered examples on Wolt: Nizoral shampoo, Strepsils, Omega-3 gummies, Magnelon, The Ordinary toner, Korres body cleansers.

## Design cues from social

- Pharmacy **green cross** / green accent branding
- Greek-language community posts (εφημερία, brand campaigns e.g. Femarelle)
- Professional education/events with pharma partners (e.g. CPO / Opella)

## Demo scope for this starter

**In scope for the website demo:** branded storefront for personal care, wellness, and cosmetics catalog + store contact/hours + cart/checkout engine.

**Out of scope / do not claim:** online sale of prescription-only medicines; replacing Wolt; official ministry duty calendar widget (link/call only).
